# Code of Conduct

CronDelta welcomes contributors with different backgrounds and levels of
experience. This policy applies to issues, pull requests, reviews, and other
project communication.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss ideas and observable behavior respectfully.
- Give actionable feedback and make room for questions and corrections.
- Respect people's privacy, boundaries, and identities.
- Keep discussion relevant to the project and resolve disagreements without
  personal attacks.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, sexual harassment, publication of
private information without consent, and repeated unwanted contact are not
acceptable. Technical disagreement is welcome; intimidation is not.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report concerns privately to the maintainer at
[zheltov_pe@mail.ru](mailto:zheltov_pe@mail.ru), with the subject
`CronDelta conduct report`. Include links or a description of the behavior and
only the personal details needed to understand it. Do not publish private reports
in an issue or pull request.

The maintainer will review reports and keep identifying information private
where possible. Information may need to be shared with people involved in
handling the report; absolute confidentiality cannot be guaranteed.

Depending on severity and repetition, maintainers may request a correction,
remove content, lock a discussion, issue a warning, or restrict participation.
Reports and responses should be handled without retaliation. If you believe a
decision missed relevant context, reply privately with that context.
